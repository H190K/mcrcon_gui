# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MCRCON GUI to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. Participants are expected to be respectful, patient and constructive.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; critique code, not people.
- Help newcomers find their footing.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly do so.

Report problems privately to **info@h190k.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
